Follow countries and crises, and read their humanitarian reports on your phone: situation reports, flash updates, appeals, assessments and maps. Reports you have opened stay readable offline.

Re.L is an unofficial app. It uses the ReliefWeb API and is not affiliated with OCHA or ReliefWeb.

<b>What it does</b>
<ul>
<li>Follow countries, crises and organizations. Their new reports land in one list.</li>
<li>Today shows the headlines chosen by ReliefWeb's editors, and what is new in what you follow.</li>
<li>Country and crisis pages open on an overview: the latest situation report, appeals, analyses, and who publishes most.</li>
<li>Reports and their PDFs open in the app. Once a PDF is on your phone, you can read it as text, search it and highlight passages.</li>
<li>The Library keeps what you saved, read and highlighted, with "Copy with citation" and a Markdown export.</li>
</ul>

<b>You need a ReliefWeb appname</b>
ReliefWeb asks every app that reads its API to use an approved name, called an appname. Re.L has none built in. Each reader requests their own from ReliefWeb, with a work email, and enters it in Settings. Without one, Re.L shows ReliefWeb's latest headlines with a short summary.

<b>Privacy</b>
No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting. The app only talks to ReliefWeb. What you follow, read and highlight stays on your phone, and so does your appname.

Re.L is free software under the GPL-3.0. Reports, PDFs and images belong to ReliefWeb and to the organizations that publish them.
