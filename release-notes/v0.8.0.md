Re.L 0.8.0, an unofficial Android app for reading ReliefWeb. Not affiliated with ReliefWeb or OCHA. Download the `.apk` file under **Assets**, below. It installs over 0.7.0 and keeps everything on your phone: your Library, highlights, what you follow and your settings.

## ReliefWeb links open in Re.L

- **Share a link to Re.L.** A colleague sends you a reliefweb.int report link by mail or in a chat: share it to Re.L and the report opens in the app, with its PDF read as text. You can save it to your Library from there.
- **What it needs.** A report already on your phone opens offline, even without an appname. Otherwise Re.L asks ReliefWeb for that one report, which needs an appname.
- **Tap a link to open it in Re.L.** On Android 12 and later, go to Settings > Reading > **Open ReliefWeb links in Re.L**. In the screen Android opens, turn on Open supported links, tap Add link and choose reliefweb.int. Before Android 12, Android asks which app should open the link.
- **Other ReliefWeb pages** (a country, a crisis, an organization) open in your browser. "Open in browser" and "Open on ReliefWeb" always go to your browser, even once Re.L opens reliefweb.int links.

## Folders in the Library

- **Sort what you saved.** Put saved reports in folders by mission or topic, such as "Chad 2027" or "ECHO proposal". A report can be in several folders. Library > **Folders** appears with your first folder.
- **Add reports** from a report's menu (**Add to folder**), or from a folder (**Add reports**). Removing a report from Saved also takes it out of its folders, with Undo.
- **Export folder** makes one Markdown file with the folder's reports and their highlights, and shares it like the export of highlights.
- **Backup.** The Library's backup file now holds your folders too. Re.L 0.7.0 and earlier can't restore a file made by 0.8.0.

## Safer with other apps

Before this version, another app on your phone could make Re.L open a report on its own and add it to your History. Now only Re.L's own alerts open a report directly. A report opened from a link that another app sends joins your History only once you scroll, tap or save it. Re.L also asks ReliefWeb at most 5 times in 10 minutes for links that come from other apps.

One known effect: an alert shown by 0.7.0 before you update, then tapped after, opens Re.L without its report.
