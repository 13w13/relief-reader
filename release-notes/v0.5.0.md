Re.L 0.5.0, an unofficial Android app for reading ReliefWeb. Not affiliated with ReliefWeb or OCHA. Download the `.apk` file under **Assets**, below.

Install it over your current version. Your saved reports, highlights, history and follows stay on your phone. If you uninstall Re.L first, they are deleted. From this version, you can also keep them in a file, with Back up your library in Settings.

## Search by organization and theme

- **Two new filters.** In Search, tap Filters. The sheet now has Organization and Theme, after Country. For example, search "cholera", then pick Sudan, MSF and Analysis.
- **Find any organization.** The 10 organizations with the most results show first, with their counts. "Search organizations" finds the others.
- Pick two organizations and you get the reports of either. Filters in different sections narrow the results, as on a country page.

## Back up your library

- **Settings > Library > Back up your library** puts your Saved reports, History, Highlights and everything you follow in one file. Android's share menu then opens, so you can send it to Drive, your email or your files.
- **The file never holds your appname**, the full text of reports, or PDFs.
- **Restore from a file**, in the same place. Pick the file, and Re.L says what it holds before anything changes. It adds to what is on the phone and never deletes. Restoring the same file twice changes nothing.
- After a restore, saved reports come back as cards and load their text when you open them. What you follow fills in at the next sync, with its last 7 days.

## Follow a regional crisis

- Some crises cross borders, such as the Sudan crisis, which reaches Chad and South Sudan. ReliefWeb's editors group these on pages of their own. In Following, tap Manage, then Follow a crisis. The regional crises come first.
- A regional crisis's documents join your Following feed, like a country's. Its page shows the editors' description and the page's lists of documents, as on ReliefWeb.
- **This is new, and built from ReliefWeb's documentation.** If Re.L can't show one of a page's lists, the page says so and links to it on ReliefWeb. If something looks wrong or missing, please [open an issue](https://github.com/13w13/relief-reader/issues).
- Regional crises need an appname, like the rest of ReliefWeb beyond the headlines.

## For slow or no connection

- **Load images.** In Settings > Reading, choose Always, Wi-Fi only or Never. It covers the photos on Today and the photos and images in reports. Without images, the headline cards keep the same height.
- **Following without an appname.** Following now lists ReliefWeb's latest headlines that name a country you follow. It says "Headlines only" above them. Add an appname in Settings to see every report for your countries.
- **What you can't read offline is greyed.** Without a connection, a report with neither its text nor its PDF on your phone shows in grey in lists. You can still tap it.
