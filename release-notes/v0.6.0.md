Re.L 0.6.0, an unofficial Android app for reading ReliefWeb. Not affiliated with ReliefWeb or OCHA. Download the `.apk` file under **Assets**, below. It installs over 0.5 and keeps everything on your phone.

## Light or dark

- **Appearance**, in Settings > Reading: System follows your phone, as before. Light and Dark keep Re.L light or dark whatever the phone does.
- It changes at once, the status bar included. On Android 12 and later, Re.L also opens in the colour you chose, without a white flash. On Android 8 to 11, with Dark chosen on a light phone, the launch screen can still flash white for a moment.

## Smaller text

- **Display has a fourth level, Smallest**, below Compact: smaller titles, tighter rows and margins, for more on each screen. Nothing changes if you don't pick it; Compact stays the default.
- **Report text goes down to 14 sp.** Aa has a sixth step, Smallest, before Smaller. Your current size stays as it is.
- **Very large system text.** Section titles such as "Headlines" no longer break in the middle of the word; the note beside them moves underneath.

## New versions on Today

- Once a day, Re.L asks GitHub for the number of its latest version. When a newer one is out, Today says "Re.L 0.6.1 is available". **See the release** opens its page; **Not now** hides it until the next version.
- The request carries no appname and nothing about you. Turn it off in Settings > About Re.L > **Check for new versions**.
- Re.L never downloads or installs anything itself. [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium) can follow the releases for you, too.

## Finding an organization

If you skipped 0.5.1: "Search organizations" now starts at 2 letters and finds the start of a word, so "solidar" finds Solidarités International. An acronym such as HI brings its organization first, and a failed search says why. For Humanity & Inclusion, type HI or humanity.
