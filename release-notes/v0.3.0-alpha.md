Test build of Re.L, an unofficial reader for ReliefWeb. Signed APK for Android 8.0 or later. It installs over 0.2.3 and keeps your saved reports, history, highlights and what you follow. The database moves to version 3, which only adds the organizations you follow.

## Follow an organization

- **Every organization on ReliefWeb has a page.** Its type, its full name, how much it publishes ("About 1 document a week on ReliefWeb · Latest: 3 Nov 2026"), the countries it covers most, where it is based, its website and the editors' description. Below that come the same filters as a country, and its documents. The app shows no logo.
- **Three ways in.** Tap an organization's name on a report. Search for it by name in Following > Manage > Follow an organization. Or pick one of the suggestions: the organizations that published analyses on your countries in the last 3 months.
- **Its documents join your Following feed,** once each. A report that arrives through Sudan and through ACAPS is one line.
- **Large organizations ask first.** Some publish more than 10 documents a week, UNHCR about 88. Following one of them offers "Reports only" by default: situation reports, analyses, assessments, appeals and evaluations. "Reports only" never hides a map that comes through a country you follow. You can switch to "Everything" in Manage.

## Today shows what is new

- Today opens on what came in since your last visit. First the new headlines, then the latest from what you follow, then Continue reading, then the latest reports on ReliefWeb from all countries.
- The week's other headlines are folded in one line ("8 earlier headlines this week"). Tap it to open them.
- A visit starts when you open Today after 30 minutes away. Reading a report and coming back keeps what is new.

## Calmer, lighter

- **Messages.** Offline, Today and the pages show one quiet line, "Offline · saved at 08:02". An error shows at the bottom of the screen for a few seconds, with a ✕ and "Retry". Slide any message sideways to close it.
- **What's new.** After an update, a short sheet lists what changed, once. Settings > About opens it again.
- **Fewer requests to ReliefWeb.** The app syncs in the background twice a day instead of four times, and skips it if you opened the app in the last 3 hours. The weekly counts refresh once a day. A report opened from a country page needs no request of its own. Most read loads 10 countries at a time. By our estimate, a reader now costs about 11 requests a day on average.
- **New icon,** the "r." in black with a red dot. The dot of "Re.L" at the top of Today is red too. Unread documents keep their blue dot.
