Test build of Re.L, an unofficial reader for ReliefWeb. Signed APK for Android 8.0 or later. It installs over 0.2.1 and keeps your saved reports, history, highlights and the countries and crises you follow. The database does not change.

## Simpler pages

- **One list, filters on top.** A country page opens on all its documents, newest first. A row of filters stays under the top bar while you scroll: All, Situation reports, Analysis, Maps, Appeals, News, Key documents, and Most read when ReliefWeb has a most-read list for that country. One filter is on at a time, and the page opens on All every time. Organizations and languages sit behind the filter icon at the end of the row.
- **Overview is a link.** "Overview" under the country's name opens the overview on its own screen, with the pinned updates on top. The page itself shows no pinned items and no document counts.
- **A lighter crisis page.** The name, the date, every affected country as a link, then "About this disaster", folded. Below that come the same filters and the list. The overview blocks are gone from crisis pages.
- **Following is one feed.** The documents of every country and crisis you follow, each document once, with a dot on what came in since your last visit. "Manage" opens the list where you add or stop following.
- **Counts match the page.** "6 new" in Today, Following and the daily summary counts every document posted since your last visit, and a document in several languages once. Open the page and you find six lines with a dot at the top. The setting that limited counts to key documents is gone.
- **Most read on ReliefWeb.** "See all" under the Most read block of Today opens the whole list ReliefWeb publishes, grouped by country and by disaster, up to five documents each. Documents already on your phone show first; the others follow as they load.
- **Smaller by default.** Lists, titles and spacing are smaller, so a screen shows about seven documents instead of three or four. Settings > Reading > Display switches between Compact, Default and Large. The same choice sits at the top of Aa in a report, above the report text sizes.
- **Less repeated information.** A row shows the format, the organization, when it was posted and the title. It adds an old period ("Covers Jul 2026, 3 months ago") and, in feeds that mix countries, the country. The PDF size, the "Read" mark and the dates line are gone. The menu of a headline in Today now opens with a long press. A report shows one line of dates.

## PDFs

- **Bullets no longer make headings in the Text view.** In a UNHCR flash update, the first line of every "▪" item showed as a heading. A list item is now never a heading, and a line cut mid-sentence stays with the rest of its paragraph. PDFs already on your phone are read again the first time you open them.
- **Limits on reading a PDF as text.** Re.L stops at 60 MB, 400 pages or 90 seconds, and before the phone runs out of memory. A long PDF read in part ends with "Text stops at page n of m" and a link to the next page in the PDF view. A PDF too large to read as text says so, and the PDF view still shows its pages.
- **PDFs over 100 MB stay on ReliefWeb.** The report says so and links to ReliefWeb's page, where your browser can download the file.

## Security

- Links taken from ReliefWeb's data, in a report or its files, open only as web or mail links.
- The libraries of the app are checked every week for updates, and the HTML parser is up to date.
