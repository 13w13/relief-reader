Test build of Relais, an unofficial reader for ReliefWeb. Signed APK for Android 8.0 or later; it installs over 0.1.0 and keeps your data.

## What changes

- Embedded fonts: Literata for titles and reading, Inter for the interface, IBM Plex Sans Condensed for section labels (all under the SIL Open Font License).
- Design fixes: headline reports open on the editors' title and summary; dates sit on separate lines (Covers, Published, Posted); Today shows the day's reading progress next to the date.
- Country page: the tabs no longer show totals, only a badge with the documents posted since your last visit.
- The five user journeys now run every night on an Android emulator (Maestro, real ReliefWeb data).

## Still to test on a real phone

- The daily summary notification, at the time chosen in Settings.
- Background sync every 6 hours with the app closed, then reading offline.
- The PDF reader: large files, pinch zoom, reopening at the last page read.
