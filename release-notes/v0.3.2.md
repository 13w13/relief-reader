Re.L 0.3.2, an unofficial Android app for reading ReliefWeb. Not affiliated with ReliefWeb or OCHA. Download the `.apk` file under **Assets**, below. It installs over 0.3.1 and keeps everything on your phone.

## Today

- **A large headline with its photo on every visit.** In 0.3.1, the large headline came back only when a headline was new since your last visit. ReliefWeb's editors publish one or two a day, so most visits had none.
- **First the new ones.** The first headline that is new and not read yet comes first, as before.
- **Then the latest.** Failing that, Today shows the most recent headline of the week under "No new headlines since your last visit", with its photo and credit. Its title is greyed if you have read it.
- **Only one photo.** Only that headline has a photo, and it doesn't come back in "earlier headlines this week".
