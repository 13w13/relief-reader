Re.L 0.4.0, an unofficial Android app for reading ReliefWeb. Not affiliated with ReliefWeb or OCHA. Download the `.apk` file under **Assets**, below.

Install it over your current version. Your saved reports, highlights, history and follows stay on your phone. If you uninstall Re.L first, they are deleted.

## Today

- **Leaf through the headlines.** ReliefWeb's headlines now come as a pile of cards, newest first. Swipe, or tap ‹ and ›. "3 of 6" under the card says where you are.
- **It opens where you stopped.** The pile starts on the first headline you haven't read.
- **One photo at a time.** Only the card on screen loads its photo. On Wi-Fi, the next card's photo loads too.
- **The end of the pile** lists this week's headlines, up to 6, with the ones you have read in grey. Below them comes "Older headlines", or "3 more this week" when the week has more than 6.
- **Older headlines.** With an appname, this opens a search of all ReliefWeb headlines by date. Without one, it lists the headlines kept on your phone, because ReliefWeb's feed gives only the 20 latest.
- **A shorter page.** After the headlines come the latest from what you follow, Continue reading, the latest reports on ReliefWeb, Most read on ReliefWeb and Most reports this week.

## Reading a headline

- A headline opened from Today ends with the title of the next one. Tap Next to read on, or Previous headline to go back.
- Back returns to Today, on the card of the last headline you read.

## What moved, and where to find it

- **The tabs on Today.** Situation reports, Analysis, Maps, News and Appeals are filters in Following and on each country page.
- **Your countries on Today.** Their new reports still show on Today under "Latest from what you follow", and all of them are in Following. Tap a country's name at the top of Following to open its page.
- **New disasters this week.** Following > Manage > Follow a crisis lists the disasters ReliefWeb tracks as alert or ongoing.
- **The "4 of 9 read" counter.** The strokes under the cards show which headlines you have read, and the end of the pile says how many are left.
- **The highlighter button.** Open the report's menu, top right, and tap Highlight.
- **The "Key documents" filter.** Pick Situation reports, Analysis or Appeals instead.
- **Countries in Settings.** Following > Manage has your countries, crises and organizations.
- **The PDF size at the bottom of the text view.** It now shows under the report's dates, with the number of pages.

## Less on screen

- **Organization page.** Three lines at the top: the name; how often it posts and when it last did; its type, where it is based and its website. What it mostly covers and ReliefWeb's description fold under "About" and its name, such as "About ACAPS".
- **Top of a report.** Four icons. Save is a bookmark, filled once the report is saved. When the report has its own text, its PDF is one line under the dates, such as "PDF · 22 pages · 352 KB".
- **PDFs as text.** The banner at the top shows only the first time.
- **Library.** The search field shows from 10 items, or as soon as a PDF is on your phone. The filter icon shows from 10 items and opens a sheet with the filters and Group by.
- **Following.** Tap a name in the line of what you follow to open its page. "+3" opens Manage.
- **Bottom bar.** The tab you came from stays lit. Open a country from Following, and Following stays lit.

## Settings

- **Changes apply at once.** Type your appname in Settings, then tap Save or the keyboard's enter key. Re.L checks it and confirms.
- **Keep history for.** A shorter choice first tells you how many reports it will remove, then removes them right away.
- **Daily summary.** The hours show only when the summary is on.

## PDFs

- **Ready offline.** On Wi-Fi, Re.L downloads the PDFs of recent reports on what you follow, and reads their text, so they open without a connection. It waits for enough battery and storage, and skips files over 25 MB. It's on by default. Turn it off in Settings > Storage > "Download PDFs on Wi-Fi".
- **Choose the space.** Pick 200 MB, 300 MB, 500 MB or 1 GB for PDFs you haven't saved. 300 MB is the default. When it's full, Re.L deletes the oldest first. PDFs of saved and highlighted reports stay. The Wi-Fi downloads fill 80% of this space at most.
- **Pages and size in lists.** A document with a PDF shows "PDF · 12 pages · 2.3 MB" under its country, when ReliefWeb gives the numbers.
- **When Android clears space.** If Android deletes a PDF to free space, Re.L forgets it too and downloads it again when you open it. Your highlights in it stay.
