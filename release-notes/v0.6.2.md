Re.L 0.6.2, an unofficial Android app for reading ReliefWeb. Not affiliated with ReliefWeb or OCHA. Download the `.apk` file under **Assets**, below. It installs over 0.6.1 and keeps everything on your phone.

## PDFs download again

Re.L 0.6.1 could not download PDFs: opening one showed "Download failed". This version fixes it. Sorry about that.

The cause: 0.6.1 changed how the app names itself to ReliefWeb's servers, and ReliefWeb's file server does not accept that form. Every request now uses `Re.L/Android`, which it accepts ([issue 36](https://github.com/13w13/relief-reader/issues/36)).

- **When a download fails, Re.L now says why**, for example "Download failed (HTTP 404)" or "(no connection)", and offers **Open in browser**.
- **Recent PDFs download again on Wi-Fi.** The PDFs the background download gave up on during 0.6.1 are tried again after this update.
