First public version of Re.L, an unofficial Android app for reading ReliefWeb. Not affiliated with ReliefWeb or OCHA. Signed APK for Android 8.0 or later: download the `.apk` file under Assets.

## What Re.L does

- **Today** opens on what came in since your last visit: new headlines, the latest from what you follow, and the latest reports on ReliefWeb.
- **Follow countries, disasters and organizations.** Their documents come together in one Following feed.
- **Read reports as text,** PDFs included. Highlight a passage, save a report, copy a quote with its citation.
- **Search** ReliefWeb, and see what ReliefWeb visitors read most.
- **A daily summary,** one notification a day, if you want it.

## Each reader brings their own appname

- **Re.L has no shared access to ReliefWeb.** Each reader asks ReliefWeb for a free appname and enters it in the app. This APK carries none.
- **"Connect to ReliefWeb" comes first,** right after the welcome screen. Enter your appname, or tap "Continue with headlines only".
- **"Request an appname"** opens the ReliefWeb API documentation, which links to the request form. The form asks for a work email. ReliefWeb replies within two working days.
- **Headlines only** shows ReliefWeb's latest headlines with a short summary. Full reports, country pages and search need an appname.
- **Your appname works at once.** After "Check and continue", Today and Search open without a restart, and the app fetches the last 7 days of what you follow.

## Updating from a test build (0.3.0)

Everything on your phone stays. This APK has no appname of its own, so "Connect to ReliefWeb" shows once after the update. Enter your appname to keep full access, or continue with headlines only and add it later in Settings > ReliefWeb access.
