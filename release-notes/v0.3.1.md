**Wake up and read the humanitarian news from ReliefWeb.** This is the first public version of Re.L, an unofficial Android app for reading ReliefWeb. Not affiliated with ReliefWeb or OCHA.

## Get it

1. Download the `.apk` file under **Assets**, below, and open it on your phone (Android 8.0 or later).
2. Open Re.L and tap **Continue with headlines only** to read ReliefWeb's latest headlines.
3. [Ask ReliefWeb for a free appname](https://apidoc.reliefweb.int/#appname) with your work email, and enter it in the app. This unlocks full reports, country pages and search.

## What you can do

**Start the day on Today**
- Today opens on what came in since your last visit: new headlines, the latest from what you follow, then the latest reports on ReliefWeb.
- It has an end. When you have read it, Re.L says you are up to date.
- "Most read on ReliefWeb" shows what ReliefWeb's visitors read most, by country and by disaster.

**Follow what matters to you**
- Follow countries, disasters and organizations. Their documents come together in one Following feed, newest first.
- Filter a feed by kind: situation reports, analyses, maps, appeals, news.
- A document posted in several languages shows once, with "Also in French".

**Read reports on a phone**
- Reports read as text, with the organization, the dates and a link to ReliefWeb.
- PDFs open inside the app, as pages or as readable text. Search inside a PDF.
- When a report covers an old period, the app says so: "Covers Jul 2026, 4 months ago".
- Choose the text size, and a compact or larger display.

**Keep what you read**
- Save a report, find it again in your Library, and pick up where you stopped.
- Highlight a passage, add a note, and copy a quote with its citation.
- Export your highlights as Markdown.

**Offline, and quiet**
- What you follow is stored on your phone and reads offline.
- One summary notification a day if you want it. Alerts for new appeals and flash updates are off until you turn them on.

## What Re.L does not do

- No AI in the app: no generated summaries, no scores.
- No ads, no account, no tracking. Re.L talks to ReliefWeb only. A report can show an image hosted on another site, and the app loads it from there.
- No shared access to ReliefWeb: each reader uses their own appname, and this APK carries none.
