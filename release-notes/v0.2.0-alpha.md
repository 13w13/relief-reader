Test build of Relais, an unofficial reader for ReliefWeb. Signed APK for Android 8.0 or later. It installs over 0.1.2 and keeps your saved reports, history, highlights and followed countries. The database moves to version 2 without a reset.

## What changes

- **Crises.** You can follow a ReliefWeb disaster next to your countries, from Following > Follow a crisis. A crisis page shows its status, its countries, the editors' description, situation reports, appeals, analyses and other disasters in the same countries. ReliefWeb does not list long conflicts such as Sudan's as disasters, so follow the country instead.
- **Overview first.** Country and crisis pages open on the latest situation report, current appeals and plans, analyses, the reports posted in the last 7 days against the 7 before, linked disasters, and who publishes most.
- **Pinned.** The top of a country or crisis shows the latest update from OCHA, or else the latest flash update, then new appeals. "Hide" sends one back to the list.
- **One line per document in several languages**, with "Also in French" under it. The app merges two versions only when they clearly match.
- **Read a PDF as text.** Once a PDF is on your phone, Relais reads its text, offline. The Text view uses the app's typography, marks each page, links to the tables and maps in the PDF view, and lets you search the PDF and highlight passages. A PDF made only of scanned images stays in the PDF view. Lists show the first lines of a PDF you already have instead of "Please refer to the attached file", and the Library search covers the PDFs you have read.
- **Select text in a report** with a long press. The bar offers Highlight, Copy with citation and Translate. The citation carries the passage, the organization, the title, the dates and the link. Translate hands the passage to the phone's translation app and is hidden when there is none.
- **Export highlights** as Markdown from Library > Highlights. The export keeps the filters you set: country, crisis, period or search.
- **Alerts for new appeals and flash updates** in what you follow. They are off by default and you turn them on in Settings. You get at most one a day for each country or crisis.
- Today has a new block, "New disasters this week".
