Re.L 0.7.0, an unofficial Android app for reading ReliefWeb. Not affiliated with ReliefWeb or OCHA. Download the `.apk` file under **Assets**, below. It installs over 0.6.2 and keeps everything on your phone: your Library, highlights, what you follow and your settings.

## A third of the size

The app file goes from 19.7 MB to about 6.6 MB. Re.L is now built with R8, a tool that removes the code the app never uses. Nothing changes in how you read.

- **Clearer download errors.** When a PDF can't be saved on your phone, Re.L now says "could not save the file". Before, it said "no connection".
- **Check now.** In Settings > About Re.L, **Check now** asks GitHub at once whether a newer version is out. If there is one, **Download the APK** opens the file in your browser, and Today's notice has **Download** too. Re.L itself never downloads or installs anything.
- **New versions show sooner.** Re.L now checks when you open it, at most once an hour, instead of once a day. **Not now** hides the notice for 3 days, and a newer version shows at once. GitHub sees your IP address at each check; Settings > About Re.L > **Check for new versions** turns it off.
- **When to update.** A release can ask older versions to update: Today then says **Please update** with **Download** until you do, and nothing is blocked. Two months after a version comes out, Today suggests checking for a newer one; this reminder uses the phone's date and contacts no one.
- **Light or dark from the start.** On a new install, the welcome screen offers Appearance: System, Light or Dark.
- **A short tour.** After the first launch setup, five pages show Today, Following, a report, highlights and Library, and what stays on your phone. Skip it at any time. Settings > About Re.L > **Show the tour again** opens it again.
- **Libraries updated**, among them Kotlin, Ktor, Room and WorkManager.

Each release now also comes with `rel-0.7.0-mapping.txt`. It turns an error report from this version back into readable names. You don't need it to install Re.L.
