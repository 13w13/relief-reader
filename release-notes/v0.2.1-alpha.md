Test build of Re.L, the new name of Relais, an unofficial reader for ReliefWeb. Signed APK for Android 8.0 or later.

## What changes

- **New name and icon.** The app is now called Re.L, with the R.L icon. The Re.L logotype sits at the top of Today.
- **It installs as a new app.** Re.L has a new application id, `org.relreader.app`, so Android keeps it apart from Relais. Saved reports, history, highlights and followed countries stay in Relais and do not move to Re.L. Pick your countries again in Re.L, then uninstall Relais when you no longer need it.
- Exports are now named `rel-highlights-...md`. Notifications show the R.L icon.

## Fixes from the first test on a phone

- **The latest situation, not the latest posted.** The overview picks the situation report that covers the most recent period. A report whose period ended more than a month ago says so under its title, for example "Covers Jul 2026, 3 months ago".
- **Pinned items that fit the crisis.** On a crisis page, OCHA's update is pinned only when it names the crisis or is about its main country.
- A crisis page lists all its countries under its name.
- Tapping the tab you are on takes you back to its first screen.
