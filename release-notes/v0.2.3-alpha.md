Test build of Re.L, an unofficial reader for ReliefWeb. Signed APK for Android 8.0 or later. It installs over 0.2.2 and keeps your saved reports, history, highlights and what you follow. The database does not change.

## Fixes from your week of use

- **Lists keep their place.** Open a report from Following, a country or crisis page, Search, the Library, Most read or Today, then go back. The list is where you left it, not at the top.
- **Messages no longer get in the way.** A message about loading, being offline or an error is one short line above the content, with a ✕ to close it. A passing error goes away by itself after a few seconds. When you are offline and the page was saved, one quiet line says so; tap it to try again.
- **Last week's headlines leave Today.** Today kept the week it had when the app started, so while the app stayed open in the background, old headlines never left the list. The week now moves on each time you come back to Today.
- **You can see when Today was updated.** "Updated 14:32" sits under the date. Pulling down now checks ReliefWeb again unless it did in the last 5 minutes. It used to wait 30 minutes, so a pull often did nothing.

ReliefWeb's editors pick only 6 to 10 headlines a week, so the headlines can stay the same after a refresh. Version 0.3 will look at how Today can show more of what is new.
