Test build of Relais, an unofficial reader for ReliefWeb. Signed APK for Android 8.0 or later; it installs over 0.1.1 and keeps your data.

## What changes

- **Pull to refresh** on Today, Following and the country page (one grouped request). Within 30 minutes of the last check it only says "Up to date".
- **Swipe between tabs** on Today, the country page and the Library.
- **Smaller article text**: 17 sp by default instead of 19, and a "Text size" setting with five steps, in the report menu and in Settings. Subheads in the text are now smaller than the title.
- **Today**: "From your countries" under the large headline shows up to 3 new situation reports, analyses or appeals of the countries you follow. "Continue reading" takes two short lines, with "See all" for your history.
- **Key documents** on country pages, as the default view: situation reports, analyses, assessments, evaluations, appeals, maps and infographics, plus any editors' headline. News releases and other documents stay in "All", with a line saying how many. A "Filters" sheet adds organization, language and theme, remembered per country. Today and Following count "6 new · 10 in all". Settings > Countries switches the default back to all documents.
- **Most read on ReliefWeb** and **Most reports this week** on Today. The first reads the site's daily file, on Wi-Fi only; the second counts reports, not needs.
- **Search**: sorted by relevance, results cover the past 12 months by default (a removable filter); Newest has no period.
- A downloaded file that is not a PDF is refused instead of being kept.
